# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profitability Insights

An executive Power BI solution transforming multi-year retail data (`2015–2018`) into actionable revenue, margin, and market intelligence across `$2.30M` in sales and `5,009` orders.

![Dashboard Preview](https://github.com/Abdullahprobro/Datheon_Data_Analytics_-_Power_BI_Internship-/blob/main/Screenshot%20(1513).png?raw=true)

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Key Terminologies

* **BI & Visualization:** Power BI Desktop (16:9 Dark-Slate Executive Theme)
* **ETL & Data Modeling:** Power Query (Null Handling, Date Parsing, Schema Standardization)
* **DAX Measures:** `Total Sales` (`$2.30M`), `Total Profit` (`$286.40K`), `Profit Margin %` (`12.47%`), `Average Order Value` (`$458.61`), `Total Orders` (`5,009`)
* **Core Visuals:** KPI Cards, Dual-Axis Line & Clustered Column Chart, AI Decomposition Tree, Regional Profitability Scatter Matrix, Conditional Market Table

---

## 🎯 Business Problem & Goal

* **Business Problem:** Leadership lacked a unified executive view to track multi-year revenue growth alongside category and regional margin erosion.
* **1. Goal of the Dashboard (Task 01 — Completed ✅):** Centralize core commercial KPIs, evaluate `2015–2018` revenue-to-profit scaling, and pinpoint top-performing categories (`Technology` at `$836.15K`) and high-margin markets on a single executive screen.
